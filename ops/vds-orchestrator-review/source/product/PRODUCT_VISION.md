# Product Vision

We are transforming Skyrim Together Reborn into a persistent multiplayer RP/MMO platform.

Skyrim Together Reborn remains the synchronization foundation. The single-player-oriented character, quest, NPC-population, and persistence assumptions are progressively replaced with server-owned multiplayer systems.

Keizaal Online and Daedric Online are structural references for the intended persistent-world experience. They are references, not code dependencies and not literal clones.

The target experience starts simply:

1. Launch Skyrim and connect.
2. Authenticate.
3. See Character Select.
4. Choose a server-owned persistent character.
5. Enter the multiplayer world.
6. Ordinary humanoid NPC population is absent; players fill humanoid roles.
7. Trusted creature/monster/undead population remains.
8. Dungeons and encounter populations are renewable and reset under server-controlled rules.
9. Disconnect/reconnect restores the server character and coherent world state.

The server owns identity, lifecycle, persistence, attribution, and trust decisions. Clients continue to simulate Skyrim runtime/AI where required. We do not invent fake server-side Skyrim AI.

Security and trust correctness beat feature velocity.
