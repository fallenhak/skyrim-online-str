# Product Vision

We are transforming Skyrim Together Reborn into a persistent multiplayer RP/MMO
platform.  Skyrim Together Reborn's stronger synchronization foundations remain
the starting point, while the single-player-oriented world and character model
is replaced with server-owned persistent multiplayer systems.

Keizaal Online and Daedric Online are structural references for the intended
persistent-world experience.  They are conceptual references only: this
product is not SkyMP source code and is not a literal clone of SkyMP, Keizaal
Online, or Daedric Online.

The server owns multiplayer identity, lifecycle, persistence, and trust
decisions.  Clients continue to simulate Skyrim runtime/AI where required, but
the product must never invent fake server-side Skyrim AI.  Security and trust
correctness beat feature velocity.
